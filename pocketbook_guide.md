# PocketBook — Minimalist Digital Stationery Reference Guide

---

## 1. Minimalist Daily Capture Syntax

In PocketBook, daily capture is designed to log thoughts and outcomes with zero friction. Every entry begins with a minimal glyph pointer that can be cycled:

| Symbol | Name | Description & Usage |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| `•` | **Task** | An actionable outcome requiring focused execution. |
| `✕` | **Completed** | A finished task. Striking it off increments your daily completion score. |
| `>` | **Deferred** | An outcome intentionally postponed to tomorrow or future spreads. |
| `<` | **Scheduled** | A commitment assigned to a future date in the Monthly Calendar. |
| `○` | **Event** | A calendar commitment, synchronous meeting, or time-locked event. |
| `—` | **Note** | Observations, research findings, meeting minutes, or ideas. |
| `★` | **Priority** | High-leverage outcomes requiring immediate cognitive focus. |

## 2. Executive Frameworks Suite

Select from 7 unencumbered, public-domain cognitive decision frameworks:

1. **Top 3**: Daily high-impact clarity focusing on the 3 vital outcomes for today.
2. **Ivy Lee Method (1918)**: 6 prioritized tasks executed strictly one at a time in numerical order.
3. **Eisenhower Matrix**: 4 decision quadrants (Do, Schedule, Delegate, Eliminate).
4. **MoSCoW Method (1994)**: Agile project triage (Must, Should, Could, Won't).
5. **ABCDE Method**: Consequence-driven alphabetical task grading.
6. **The 1-3-5 Rule**: 1 Big, 3 Medium, 5 Small tasks for balanced workload management.
7. **Pareto 80/20 (1896)**: Concentrating energy on the vital 20% driving 80% of results.

## 3. Contextual Category Markers & Color Codes

Tasks and notes can be tagged with contextual category indicators:

- 🔵 **Professional**: Strategic work, engineering, architecture, executive alignment.
- 🟢 **Personal**: Health, fitness, wellness, family, finances.
- 🟠 **Travel**: Transit, flights, itineraries, logistical planning.
- 🟣 **Creative**: Writing, design exploration, conceptual ideation.
- 🍷 **Urgent**: Immediate blockers, time-sensitive interventions.

## 4. The Daily Journaling Cadence

1. **Morning Alignment (2 Minutes)**:
   - Review previous entries and migrate pending tasks.
   - Choose your framework and specify your key outcomes.
   - Select your archival fountain pen ink and paper tone.
2. **Throughout the Day (Continuous Capture)**:
   - Capture tasks (`•`), meetings (`○`), and observations (`—`) instantly.
   - Tag categories and strike items off upon completion (`✕`).
3. **Evening Reflection (2 Minutes)**:
   - Complete your habit streak ticks.
   - Note down a 1-line evening reflection and close out your day with peace of mind.

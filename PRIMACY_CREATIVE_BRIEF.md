# Primacy — Product Experience And Launch Brief

## Production Prompt

Redesign and implement Primacy as a precise, calm productivity instrument. The audience is a person who needs to decide what matters, make progress without constantly reorganizing their work, and see how daily action contributes to longer-term direction. Explain the product immediately. Lead with an understandable benefit rather than an abstract slogan.

### Message And Naming

Use **Primacy** as the working masterbrand and **The Productivity Instrument** as the descriptor. Proposed opening: **“Choose your priorities. Start with what matters.”** Follow with: “Primacy is your personal prioritization instrument. List your tasks. Choose a framework. Decide what comes first.” Make the product tangible with “Your Priorities. Your Work.” Use research-informed wording, link relevant primary research, and distinguish research behind an approach from evidence that Primacy itself improves outcomes. Never promise scientifically proven product performance. Do not use the two excluded category words in visible copy, accessibility labels, metadata, or rendered page textures.

Use a simple naming architecture: Primacy → Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly → Priorities / Notes / Habits / Reflection / Decisions / Progress. Use “Approach” for choosing a prioritization framework, “Tools” for occasional utilities, and “Settings” for configuration. Tabs and controls are Title Case. Explain specialist frameworks in plain language. Keep persisted IDs compatible when changing labels. Evaluate alternate masterbrand names as unverified candidates, not legally cleared recommendations.

### Art Direction

Use a strict neutral black-and-white landing page with neutral gray for hierarchy. Remove cream, gold, tan, and warm off-white accents. Center the Primacy wordmark precisely regardless of navigation widths. Preserve spacious, editorial typography, but keep the first-screen object visibly large and recognizably proportioned as a substantial open notebook. Pages are clean optical white, ink is crisp black, cover is black, and stitching and paper edges are visible. Avoid blown-out illumination, soft texture sampling, excessive depth of field, fake fog, and an undersized flattened object.

### Hero And Object Rendering

Restore the generous earlier object presentation. Produce detailed, sharply resolved render assets and improve the interactive WebGL object. Use restrained studio lighting, realistic paper curvature at the spine, fine paper laminations, a sharply woven fabric label, stitched binding, rounded black cover corners, and contact shadows. Use high-resolution text textures without invented UI or unreadable decorative text. Show authentic product content using isolated sample data. Keep interfaces black ink on white pages. Use sufficient antialiasing and texture anisotropy; respect GPU limits. Do not call an asset 4K or 8K without inspecting its actual dimensions.

Animation should feel weighty, fluid, and controlled: damped rotation, eased camera moves, small deliberate idle motion, smooth transitions between overview and detail, and no scroll capture. Provide a global animation pause, reduced-motion handling, keyboard rotation, touch scrolling, and a static fallback. Use pre-rendered high-detail imagery for photographic art direction and genuine 3D geometry for user-controlled viewpoints; describe the distinction honestly.

### Product View Explorer

Create a clearly labeled interactive section for Daily, Monthly, and Yearly perspectives (include Weekly where helpful). For each selected perspective offer Front View, Angled View, and Close-Up. Animate the camera/object gently between these views, show visibly different sample page content, and explain the user benefit of the selected perspective. A close-up must expose useful content or construction detail. Preserve selected perspective when changing view. Controls must work by keyboard and fit on small screens without horizontal page overflow.

### Device Presentation

Use detailed MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, and iPhone Pro presentation mockups: realistic aluminum edges, correctly proportioned screens, bezels, rounded corners, camera cutouts, keyboard/trackpad details for the laptop, side controls and camera details where visible. Show Primacy's crisp white interface on each. Use original generated artwork/procedural assets, not copied Apple promotional files. These are illustrative compatibility mockups; do not imply affiliation, endorsement, exact current-generation CAD, or verified performance on untested hardware. Add tasteful hover/scroll movement and explicit front/angle/close-up views where appropriate. Validate proportions and screen legibility at actual page size.

### Product UX

Keep the daily work surface focused on clear priorities and a next action. Place Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly in one stable navigation row with centered branding. Group secondary actions under Tools. Eliminate redundant navigation and clipped utility strips. Retain existing data, editing, framework selection, habits, reflection, search, export, privacy, and accessible controls. Do not invent a completed payment backend or encrypted storage guarantee. Preserve normal landing-page vertical scrolling across reloads and workspace transitions.

### Legal And Commercial Review

Review method names, masterbrand naming, visual references, Apple product naming/artwork, fonts, open-source dependencies, generated assets, research claims, privacy, analytics, consumer disclosures, taxes, and payment/licensing design. Use current authoritative sources. Record confirmed observations separately from questions requiring jurisdiction-specific professional review; a source-code scan is not legal clearance. Assess India-first payment options and international merchant-of-record options without assuming vendor onboarding approval.

### Deployment And Architecture

Build and validate the prototype, then deploy to the existing Sites project under its verified owner-only access. Do not accept real payments through the demo checkout. Recommend a staged production architecture with static frontend delivery, a server-side payment/order boundary, verified idempotent webhooks, durable entitlements, no secrets in the client, account recovery, export/backups, and optional authenticated synchronization. Explain local browser storage limitations and how moving to a hosted domain affects access to existing local data.

### Acceptance Criteria

1. Hero explains what Primacy is and why a user would use it.
2. Black/white/neutral-gray palette; optical-white rendered pages; centered logo; Title Case tabs.
3. Large, sharp, properly proportioned object with visible material details and fluid motion.
4. Daily/Monthly/Yearly and Front/Angled/Close-Up controls show meaningful visual changes.
5. Detailed device mockups labeled MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, and iPhone Pro, with honest illustration disclosure.
6. Wheel/touch/keyboard navigation works without landing scroll lock; narrow-screen layouts have no horizontal overflow.
7. Existing workspace functionality and stored data are preserved.
8. Build and appropriate checks pass; console/render issues and material limitations are documented.
9. Deliver a deployed prototype URL, source-backed naming/legal/architecture review, final asset paths, and the exact creative prompt.

## Accepted Positioning Correction

The central job is prioritization, not planning or doing work on the user’s behalf. The individual lists tasks, chooses a framework, identifies top priorities, and does the work. Secondary habits/reflection/perspectives support that primary job. Replace the demo dropdowns with directly clickable, accessible icon choices and a visible explanation of the selected approach.

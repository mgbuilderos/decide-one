# Mobile design audit — 15 September 2026

The founder-approved direct instrument and open book are the design baseline. The full tool is free; conversion here means a reader opening it and making a useful choice, not an immediate payment. The payment gateway remains outside this pass.

1. **Month view, first use and short phone.** The earlier six-row grid wasted a week in September and compressed dates on a 320 × 568 screen until they were unreadable. The current grid uses the number of weeks the month needs, keeps 44px month controls, and places the selected-day field above the dates on short screens. The month itself scrolls internally when five or six readable rows cannot fit. [390px before](01-month-mobile.png), [390px after](01-month-mobile-updated.png), [320px before](09-month-tiny-updated.png), [320px after](13-month-tiny-editor-above.png), [320px last date reached](10-month-tiny-final-bottom.png).
2. **Weekly record.** An empty week used to say every commitment was completed, although none was entered. It now says no priorities were entered and gives no completion percentage. The phone pages use “This Week” and “Review”; their controls are larger and can wrap at 320px. [Before](03-week-summary-mobile.png), [after](03-week-summary-mobile-updated.png), [320px after](08-week-tiny-updated.png).
3. **Educational entry and sharing.** A guide had no clear way to open the instrument above its long article. A 44px top link now opens the free daily page. The former share image showed retired features; social metadata now points at a crisp capture of the actual open book with a task and planned time. [Guide before](06-guide-mobile.png), [guide after](06-guide-mobile-updated.png), [current desktop surface](12-desktop-day-source.png), [retired share image](retired-social.jpg).

Other reviewed phone states: [daily](02-day-mobile.png), [weekly review](04-week-review-mobile.png), [yearly](05-year-mobile.png), [Matrix](matrix-mobile.png). The day page keeps time beside the priority that owns it and the yearly index fits twelve months without sideways scrolling.

Automated source, build, published-asset and Chrome layout checks pass. The Chrome checks cover empty, written, running, paused and closed day states; desktop, phone and 320px sizes; contrast, clipping, console errors and keyboard routing. This is not a manual screen-reader audit, real-device performance test, or evidence of a measured conversion uplift. The live site was not published in this pass.

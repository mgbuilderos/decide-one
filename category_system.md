## 3. How the Marker System Works in the App

### 3.1 Dual Representation: Bullet Marker + Pill Badge
1. **The Bullet Marker**: The bullet pointer itself (`•`, `✕`, `>`, `○`, `—`) is enclosed in a circular halo colored with the category's signature ink color.
2. **The Category Pill**: An elegant, subtle tag displays the emoji and label:
   ```
   • [ 💼 Work ] Finalize API design documentation
   ✕ [ 📚 Read ] Chapter 4 of 'Deep Work' by Cal Newport
   • [ ✈️ Travel ] Check-in for flight AI-102 & download boarding pass
   • [ 🏋️ Health ] 5km morning run + stretching
   • [ 🛍️ Shop ] Organic almond milk & coffee beans
   ```

### 3.2 Smart Auto-Association (Zero-Effort Tagging)
When a user begins typing a task:
- If they type *"Read 20 pages of Atomic Habits"*, the system recognizes the word *"Read"* and automatically pre-selects the **📚 Learning** marker!
- If they type *"Flight to Paris packing list"*, it detects *"Flight"* and assigns **✈️ Travel**!
- If they type *"Buy groceries at Whole Foods"*, it detects *"Buy/groceries"* and assigns **🛍️ Shopping**!
- The user can always override or change the marker with a single click from a mini emoji popover.

### 3.3 Powerful Grouping & Sorting Modes

Users can switch the Daily Log view into 3 distinct viewing modes via the toolbar:

1. **Chronological / Free-Flow Mode (Default BuJo)**:
   - Items appear in the natural order logged during the day.
2. **Group by Category Mode**:
   - Groups tasks under clean notebook headers with colored divider ribbons:
     - ⚡ **Top 3 Hard Tasks**
     - 💼 **Professional (3)**
     - 🌿 **Personal & Health (2)**
     - ✈️ **Travel (1)**
     - 🛍️ **Shopping & Errands (2)**
3. **Filter by Category Mode**:
   - Quick-filter chips at the top of the notebook page (`All`, `💼 Work`, `🌿 Personal`, `✈️ Travel`, `📚 Read`, `🏋️ Health`, etc.).
   - Clicking `💼 Work` dims or hides other categories so knowledge workers can achieve 100% focused tunnel vision during deep work blocks!

## 4. Category Balance Analytics (The "Life Harmony" Metric)

A major flaw of traditional to-do lists is that they give users false productivity signals: someone may check off 15 work tasks while completely neglecting their health, sleep, and relationships.

Our **Productivity & Life Balance Dashboard** tracks:
- **Category Distribution**: A colorful visual breakdown (e.g. 50% Work, 20% Health, 15% Learning, 15% Personal).
- **Life Balance Insights**:
  - *"Great job prioritizing Health & Fitness today! 🏋️"*
  - *"All 3 Hard Tasks were completed in Professional & Deep Work! 💼"*
  - *"Consider setting a Personal or Health task for tomorrow to maintain balance."*

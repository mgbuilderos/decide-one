The three methods

  • The Top 3 method — three things for today and no fourth, for a day holding more than it can
  • The Ivy Lee method — six tasks in strict order, given to Charles Schwab at Bethlehem Steel in 1918
  • The urgent/important matrix — sorting on two axes before deciding what to do
